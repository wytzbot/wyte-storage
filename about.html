About WYTE Vault

WYTE remembers the things you don't.

WYTE Vault exists for one reason: the boring, easy-to-forget stuff — warranty windows, subscription renewals, return deadlines, document expiries — costs people real money and real stress. WYTE keeps it all in one private place and tells you when it matters, so you don't have to hold it in your head.

Privacy-first, by design

Your vault lives on your device by default. We built WYTE local-first on purpose: your receipts and documents are yours, and they don't need to leave your phone for the app to work.
