Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE — fill in before publishing]

The short version

Your vault stays on your device unless you choose a feature that requires syncing. WYTE Vault is built local-first: your receipts, documents and item details are stored in your browser or app storage, not uploaded to a server, unless you explicitly turn on a sync feature we add in the future.

What we store locally

Item names, categories, dates, prices, notes, and any receipt or document attachments you add. This data lives in local storage on your device and is not transmitted anywhere by the core app.

What we don't collect

We do not access your bank accounts, read your messages, or upload your documents to our servers as part of normal use. WYTE Vault is not a financial-advice application and does not analyze your finances beyond what you type in.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, reminders are scheduled based on the dates you enter. If this app is later packaged for app stores, push delivery is handled by OneSignal via Median.co, which may process a device identifier and notification content in order to deliver the message.

Advertising

Free-tier users may see banner ads served by a third-party ad network. Ad networks may use identifiers to serve and measure ads. Upgrading to Pro removes ads entirely.

Payments

Pro subscription payments are processed by a third-party payment provider. We do not store your card details. Subscription status is confirmed through our payment provider's webhook.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled in a given build, we may collect basic, non-identifying usage data (such as which screens are opened) to understand how the app is used. This does not include the contents of your vault.

Data retention & deletion

Local data stays on your device until you delete it, clear your browser/app storage, or use "Clear local vault data" in Settings. You can export your data at any time from Settings → Vault → Export My Vault.

Security

Because your vault is stored locally by default, its security depends in part on your device's own security (passcode, disk encryption, etc). We recommend keeping your device locked and up to date.

Children's privacy

WYTE Vault is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect data from children.

International users

WYTE Vault is designed to work internationally. Pricing is currently shown in Nigerian Naira (₦); additional currencies may be added later.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
