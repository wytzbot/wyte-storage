FAQ

What is WYTE Vault?

A private place to keep track of purchases, warranties, subscriptions, returns and important documents — so you stop losing receipts and missing deadlines.

What can I store?

Receipts, warranties, return windows, subscriptions, documents like passports or licenses, and any other date-based reminder that matters to you.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Attachments are stored locally on your device, not on a remote server, unless you explicitly use a future cloud-sync feature.

Does WYTE access my bank account?

No. WYTE Vault never connects to your bank accounts.

Does WYTE give financial advice?

No. It's a personal organization and reminder tool, not a financial-advice app. It won't recommend investments, loans, or insurance products.

How do reminders work?

Each item can have a reminder schedule (e.g. 30, 7, and 1 day before its date). WYTE checks your items and lets you know when something's coming up.

Why didn't I receive a notification?

In-browser notifications only fire while the app is open in most browsers. Real device push notifications (even with the app closed) require the app to be installed via Median.co with OneSignal configured — check Settings → Notifications for your current status.

How do I enable notifications?

Go to Settings → Notifications → Enable Notifications, and allow the permission prompt from your browser or device.

Can I use WYTE without an account?

Yes. Version 1 works fully without creating an account — everything is stored locally.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Settings → Vault → Export My Vault downloads a JSON file you can keep or import back later.

How does Pro work?

WYTE Pro is ₦3,500/month and removes ads, unlocks unlimited items, and adds advanced reminders and filtering.

How do I cancel Pro?

Manage or cancel your subscription from Settings → Subscription, through the payment provider used at checkout.

What happens if my subscription expires?

Your account moves back to the Free plan. Your data isn't deleted — you may just be limited by Free-tier limits again.

How do I contact support?

support@wytevault.app or Settings → Support.
